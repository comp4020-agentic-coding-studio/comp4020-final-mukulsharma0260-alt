## Arrival film and its music

The 30-second film shown on first entry is a video, shown full-screen with no
sound. The arrival's galaxy image (above) sits behind it and shows only until
the video starts playing.

- **Filename:** `public/assets/arrival-film-1080.webm` and
  `arrival-film-1080.mp4`, made from the supplied file
  `Time feels this fast when you live from the beginning to an end.SFX - @kerolosmechail #creative .mp4`
- **Source:** AI-generated by Mukul Sharma using Luna AI
- **Date:** 18 October 2026
- **Use:** Cinematic anonymous-arrival visual for Overlap
- **Licence:** Original AI-generated work supplied by the project author for
  this public university assessment; the tutor has approved its use
- **Description:** a montage from the origin of the universe to the end of a
  life: galaxies, an eye, light, a lotus, DNA, golden particles and a falling
  figure.

The video is used in its original order, with a few shots taken out. Removed
(frames counted at 30 fps in the original): the opening X-ray skulls and
silhouettes on black (0–15), the screaming and distorted faces (75–87), the
walking silhouettes on black (210–213), the nude Vitruvian Man, screaming
faces and sketched skulls (218–235), and the glitch and black ending
(303–318). The remaining 8.4 seconds are slowed to fill the 30-second film.

The original cuts hard between scenes, several times a second, and swings
between near-white and near-black frames, which breaks the rule against
content that flashes more than three times a second. So each cut is softened
into a short dissolve of about 0.4 seconds and the brightest whites are
toned down a little. It is then upscaled to 1920×1080 (Lanczos, with light
sharpening) and encoded as H.264 and VP9. The original's audio is removed
entirely: the shipped files have no audio track. `spec/media.test.ts` checks
both the missing audio and the flash limit.

Its soundtrack is a music file:

- **Filename:** `public/assets/arrival-film-music.mp3`
- **Source:** Original music created by Mukul Sharma
- **Date:** 18 October 2026
- **Use:** 30-second anonymous arrival-film soundtrack for Overlap
- **Licence:** Original work supplied by the project author for this public
  university assessment
- **Description:** a soft instrumental that builds from a quiet opening to a
  steady swell.

The original is about 61 seconds long. The shipped file is its first 30
seconds, re-encoded at 128 kbps with a 2-second fade at the end and its
metadata removed. No other recording, sample or song is used.

Two other supplied files are not used:

- `lounge-transition.mp4`: an AI-generated warp/starburst clip supplied by
  the project author on 17 October 2026. Its look (bursts, a ring portal,
  fast streaks) is the opposite of a calm arrival, so it is not shipped.
- `lounge-ambience.mp3`: a commercial song saved from social media. It is
  never used.

Both are kept, unshipped, in the gitignored `assets-src/unsourced/`. The
original Luna video, with its audio, is kept unshipped in the gitignored
`assets-src/luna/`.

## Break music

Five audio files were supplied by the project owner on 18 October 2026 as
permitted non-copyright audio for the Break drawer. None of the files carry
any artist, title, licence or source in their embedded metadata (only the
encoder that wrote them), so no creator or licence is recorded here beyond
that statement. Nothing below is invented.

| Supplied file | Shipped as | Break mode | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `calm.mp3` | `public/assets/audio/calm.mp3` | Calm | 11:56 | Soft, slow; two quiet passages (about 10 s and 6 s) |
| `focus.mp3` | `public/assets/audio/focus.mp3` | Focus | 3:35 | Steady; the original clipped (true peak +4.1 dBTP) |
| `energy.mp3` | `public/assets/audio/energy.mp3` | Energy | 0:54 | Bright and dense; mono |
| `horror.mp3` | `public/assets/audio/horror.mp3` | Deep Space | 3:14 | Dark and wide; offered as "Deep Space", never labelled horror |
| `beat sk.mp3` | `public/assets/audio/beat-sk.mp3` | Night Walk | 0:51 | A low steady beat; renamed so the URL has no space |

Each shipped copy was made from the supplied original with ffmpeg: a two-pass
loudness normalisation to -16 LUFS integrated, a true-peak limiter so no
track clips (all now peak below -0.9 dBTP), re-encoded as VBR MP3 (LAME
quality 5) at 44.1 kHz with the original channel count, and all metadata
removed. So switching mode never jumps in volume. `spec/break.test.ts`
checks the loudness, the peak and the missing metadata of every shipped
track.

The supplied originals are kept, unshipped, in the gitignored
`assets-src/break/`.
