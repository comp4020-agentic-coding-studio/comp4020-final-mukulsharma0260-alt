# Terms

Everything a visitor reads uses these words, and only in these senses.

- **lounge** (The Lounge): the whole anonymous shared place, the product
  itself. Its top bar is the same in every view.
- **Common Room**: the default view inside the lounge, one full-screen field
  where every real star is shown. It is where a visitor arrives after the
  film. It is the one place called a room, and it is not a chat room or
  channel.
- **constellation**: one of five fixed, optional subject spaces inside the
  lounge (Science, Engineering, Business, Health, Arts & Society). Each has
  its own full-screen view, reached from the top bar, a doorway in the Common
  Room, or its address (`#science`, `#engineering`, `#business`, `#health`,
  `#arts-society`). A constellation is never called a room.
- **star**: one visitor's persistent mark. A visitor has at most one. A star
  is either in the Common Room only, or in one constellation, and a star in a
  constellation is shown in the Common Room too. It is never two stars.
- **glowing** / **studying now**: a visitor has entered the lounge and has a
  visible tab. "N studying now" counts glowing stars; "N studying in
  Engineering now" counts glowing stars in that constellation.
- **recent**: a star that is not glowing but was seen within the last day.
- **past trace** / **trace**: a star seen longer ago. "N traces" counts every
  star, glowing or not (in a constellation's view, only its own).
- **you**: the ring and label on the visitor's own star.
- **Break**: the small music drawer. "N listening now" counts glowing stars
  that pressed Play, and only appears when it is above zero.
- **callsign**: the name a visitor chooses for their messages to appear
  under (ADR 0012). It is an anonymous alias, never a real name, never proof
  of a real person, and belongs to the browser identity, not to a star: a
  visitor may have one with no star, or a star with none.
- **conversation**: the chat drawer's own word for itself. "Common Room
  conversation" names the one Common Room conversation; a constellation's
  conversation is named the same way ("Engineering conversation"), never
  called a "chat room," so the existing rule that a constellation is never
  called a room still holds.

Words never used for the place: server, channel, sky (as a name), check-in,
profile, followers. "Sky" may describe the picture, never name the place.
"Observatory" was an earlier prototype's name and is not visitor-facing.
